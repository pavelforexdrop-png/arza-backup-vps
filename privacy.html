Privacy and Google data use

ARZA Backup VPS is a personal utility operated by the owner of the ARZA server. This notice describes the utility's Google Drive integration.

Access and purpose. The utility requests only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. It checks the authorized account's email address, storage quota, and the ownership and sharing of its backup folder and files. It uploads encrypted recovery archives, reads their metadata and downloads their encrypted bytes to verify checksums. Google Drive receives encrypted archives; the decryption key is not uploaded.

Storage and protection. The OAuth client configuration and renewable Google authorization are stored in a protected configuration directory on the owner's server, accessible only to its administrator. They are excluded from recovery archives. The utility uses HTTPS for Google API requests. Decryption credentials are held separately by the owner.

Sharing and retention. Backup files remain private to the authorized account. The utility does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, disclose it to an analytics service or use it to train AI models. Cloud archives are retained until the owner removes them. A limited cache of verified encrypted duplicates remains on the server; unuploaded copies are retained.

Control. The owner can stop the backup timer, revoke the utility's access in their Google Account, remove its server-side OAuth configuration, and manage backup files in Google Drive. Revoking access stops future cloud operations and does not delete existing archives. The owner manages recovery keys and administrator access.

ARZA Backup VPS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements where applicable.

This page contains no account data or recovery credentials. Updated: 31 October 2026.
